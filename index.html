    This guide shows Western Heritage staff how to update the store. Most changes happen in the BigCommerce admin or in Page Builder. No code is needed.

    To find a topic, type a word in Search at the top of the contents, for example newsletter or gallery. Click a result to go to that section; the words you searched for are highlighted there. Press / to jump to the search box, and Esc to clear it. On a phone, tap Contents first.

    Before you start

    You edit the store in two places.

    What you changeWhere
    Products, categories, prices, logo, web pagesBigCommerce admin
    Home page sections and the main menu dropdownsPage Builder (Storefront › My Themes › Edit in Page Builder)

    Page Builder shows the page as customers see it. Click a section to edit it. Changes are private until you click Publish.

    Never do these

    • Never click "Update … URLs" in Settings › URL Structure. It changes the address of every product and category at once. Old links and search results break.
    • Never edit option sets (Products › Option Sets). Their price rules have no backup.
    • Never click Apply on another theme in Storefront › Themes. The live store switches to that theme at once.

    Words used in this guide

    • Widget: a section you add in Page Builder, such as a banner or a column of links.
    • Drop zone: an area in Page Builder that holds widgets. Its name shows in a blue label.
    • Every page: a drop zone whose widgets show on all pages of the store.

    Widgets

    Use this list to pick the right widget. In Page Builder, our widgets are under CUSTOM. Each one below shows its name as it appears there, and a screenshot of how it looks on the store.

    Use only the CUSTOM widgets. Collapse the BASIC group and leave it alone. If a Basic widget is added by mistake, it takes the store's fonts, link color and button shape, but it doesn't have the layout, backgrounds or options of our widgets. Replace it with the matching CUSTOM widget.

    A new widget starts with Lorem Ipsum text and tan placeholder photos showing their size, for example 1200 × 900. Replace all of them before you publish. Links, numbers and years start with real values. Check them too. Testimonials is the exception: it starts with the quotes from the home page.

    Page widgets

    Blog Posts: the newest 3 or 6 blog posts.

    Call to Action: a dark box with a heading, two buttons, a short line under the buttons and a photo. The line is an email link, or, with the email address left empty, any short text such as your hours. Text on its own sits centred under the buttons, or at the left (Email line › position). Don't put text in the Email address box: it becomes an email link. To leave the photo out, set Photo to None in the Photo section; the text then uses the whole box.

    Chapter: one part of a story. Small heading, large gold year, heading, text, up to 4 blocks, buttons, social icons, a sign-off, and a photo or video.

    Collection: 4 products from a category, or the SKUs you list.

    Craft Video: text beside a YouTube video, then up to 4 points with icons.

    Craft Video with Text color › Dark, for light backgrounds:

    Crew: heading and text, a group photo, and up to 10 people with a round photo, name and role.

    Custom Design: the "Design your own" section, with tags, buttons, a photo and 4 steps.

    FAQ List: one group of questions that open to show their answer, under a small heading and a heading. Up to 15 questions. Type each question in the side panel and its answer on the page, under the question (bold, italic, links and new paragraphs work). Each answer can have a photo with a caption under it. In Page Builder every answer shows, so you can type in it; on the store they open as set in Questions. On the FAQ page, use one FAQ List per group and type a Section link name (Additional), for example ordering. A Tag Links widget at the top of the page with links such as #ordering then jumps to each group.

    Feature Band: a dark band with a photo, text, buttons, logos and links.

    Featured: a carousel of products.

    Gallery: one large photo beside four small ones.

    Help Links: a heading and up to 4 links, each with an icon in a gold square.

    Hero: a large photo with a heading, a paragraph, two buttons and an optional small gold link under them (Buttons › Small gold link, e.g. "Looking for a gov. agency? Find yours here"). A new Hero is Short, the height for inner pages. On the home page, set Layout › Height to Tall. Heading level (under Text): H1 when the Hero heading is the page title, as on inner pages; H2 otherwise. Background › Image width › Right half puts the photo on the right and fades it into the background color on the left, as on the About page. The photo is never cropped at the sides; on smaller screens it gets smaller instead, so everyone in it stays visible and clear of the text. Use a wide photo (about 3 times as wide as it is tall) with the people in its right half. The Hero photo is hidden on tablets and phones, where the text covers most of it. To show it there, set Background › Image on tablets and phones to Show.

    Background › Cutout photo in front puts a second photo in front of the background, as on the About page: the brick wall is the Image and the crew is the cutout. The cutout must be a PNG with a see-through background. It sits at the bottom right and is never cropped; on smaller screens it gets smaller, so it stays clear of the text. Darken image only darkens the background, not the cutout. While a cutout is set, the background image always fills the full width (Image width is ignored). The cutout is hidden on tablets and phones.

    Icon Cards: an optional centred heading and up to 4 cards with an icon, title, text and dotted link. Each card can use its own icon: upload a PNG or SVG under Icon image, or paste SVG code. See Trust Bar icons; it works the same way. The icon shows in rust on light cards and tan on outlined cards. Layout › Cards has three looks: light cards with cut corners (About "Today"), white cards with cut corners (Customized Items "A few more ways we can help."; up to 340 wide and centred), or outlined on dark.

    Info Cards: up to 8 cards with a photo, label, title and text. Choose 3 or 4 per row, or 3 smaller cards, centred (as in Finishes & extras), white or outlined cards. With 7 or 8 cards, the widget always shows 4 per row. Card layout › Small, icon on the left shows small cards with no photo, such as Solid Gold and White Bronze on the custom buckle page. Give each card an icon: paste SVG code, or upload a one-color PNG or SVG. It shows in rust. The badge sits in the top right corner.

    Info Tabs: a box on a photo with a heading and up to 3 tabs. Each tab has text, an optional table and up to 4 short sections. Used for Before You Order on product pages.

    Newsletter: an email signup, with an optional first name box and a checkbox ("I want to subscribe to your mailing list") that must be ticked. Turn the checkbox off or change its text under Signup form.

    Photo & Links: a photo beside a list of links. Leave a link's URL empty to show it as plain text, with no arrow.

    Photo Cards: photo cards that link to parts of the store. Large shows up to 4 big cards. Compact shows up to 8 small ones.

    Photo Feature: text beside a photo or a video, with up to 4 points and two buttons. The paragraphs and point text are typed on the page. Use Light text on dark backgrounds. Set Width to Box to keep the background inside the page width.

    Steps: up to 4 numbered steps, then a one-line note with a link.

    Story & FAQs: a short story with a portrait on the left, questions that open on the right. An optional wide photo can go above the story.

    Tag Links: up to 16 rounded links.

    Testimonials: one customer quote at a time, with arrows.

    Text & Button: a short call to action after a section: top text, a heading, text, up to two buttons and bottom text. Every part is optional. Leave one empty and it takes no room, so the same widget can be just a button, a heading and a button, or all five parts. Type the top text, text and bottom text on the page (bold, italic and links work); the heading and buttons go in the side panel. Layout sets centred or left, and how wide the text runs. It starts with no space above, so it sits close under the section before it. Change that in Spacing. Use the same background color as the section above.

    Text & Photo: text with a large gold number and up to 4 figures, beside a photo. To show several photos as a slideshow, add them under More photos (slideshow) (up to 6 in all, each with a description). Visitors move through them with the arrows, the dots or a swipe. Move to the next photo by itself turns them every 5, 7 or 10 seconds; it pauses while the pointer is on the photo. To make a photo clickable, fill in its Photo link. Turn on Open in new tab to open the link in a new tab. The photo frame always keeps the same landscape shape (about 7:5). Each photo fills the frame and is cropped at the edges, whatever its own shape. Keep the subject near the centre.

    Timeline: up to 6 dates on a line with gold dots. Each date can link to a section further down the page. It is hidden on tablets and phones; to show it there, set Additional › On tablets and phones to Show.

    Trust Bar: a bar with up to 6 short points, each with an icon. Style: Dark bar, or Light box (the info bar on product pages).

    Menu widgets

    These go in the menu dropdowns. See Edit the main menu.

    Menu Links: a column of links. Style changes how it looks: Shop by (grey column at the left), Standard, or Short list.

    Menu Products: products from a category, or the SKUs you list.

    Menu Banner: a photo with a heading and a button.

    Menu Photo: a tall photo at the left edge of a dropdown.

    Menu Tiles: a category's subcategories as tiles.

    Menu Top Link: sets the name of a top-level menu item, such as Belt Buckles, and where it goes when it is clicked. Drag it into that item's dropdown. Under Top menu item link, type the name and the address, for example /belt-buckles/. Customers don't see the widget. In Page Builder it shows as a small note at the top of the dropdown. Leave a field empty to keep the name or link set in the theme. Use one per dropdown. Keep names short: all seven items must fit on one row.

    Footer widgets

    Footer About: the name, address, up to two phone numbers, email and hours.

    The footer's link columns are Menu Links widgets.

    Footer Bar: the line at the bottom with short items and the copyright.

    Footer Badge: a badge at the right end of the link columns. It shows the USFCR Verified Vendor badge unless you upload another image. Place it last in the Footer drop zone. You can add a link and change its width. Below the badge are the Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube and Instagram icons. Change their links under Social links. Leave a link empty to hide that icon. The icons open in a new tab.

    Edit the main menu

    Everything inside the menu dropdowns is edited in Page Builder. One edit changes the menu on every page.

    Open a dropdown

    1. Open Page Builder.
    2. In the Page list at the top left, pick Edit Menu. This page exists only for editing the menu. It is not linked anywhere.
    3. Scroll below the menu bar. On this page all six dropdowns show open, one under the other. On every other page they stay closed in Page Builder, and on the live store they open on hover.
    4. Each dropdown has a blue label, for example "Belt Buckles menu · every page". That is the dropdown's drop zone.
    5. Click a widget inside it to edit it. Its settings open on the left.
    6. Click Save, then Publish. The change shows on every page.

    Clicking a menu item does nothing in Page Builder. Always use the Edit Menu page.

    Menu Banner and Menu Photo have an Image description (alt text) field. Describe the photo in a few words, for people who use screen readers.

    Change a link

    1. Click the column that holds the link.
    2. Open Top of column: links or Bottom of column: links.
    3. Change the link text or the URL.
    4. To remove a link, delete its text.

    Store pages use a short URL, such as /belt-buckles/. Copy it from the address bar. Links to other websites start with https://. They open in a new tab.

    Every link and button has an Open in new tab switch under its address. Turn it on to open that link in a new tab. Leave it off for normal store links.

    Every home page section has a Background group at the bottom of its settings. Choose a background color from the brand colors, or set it to None to show the page's paper texture. You can also add a background image:

    Paper texture lays the page's paper grain over the background color, as on the sand and green sections of the design. It is on in Photo Cards, Feature Band and Testimonials. Turn it on in any other section to match. Use it with Sand or Forest green for the exact colors of the design.

    • Image size: Fill the section for a photo. Repeat for a texture.
    • Image strength: lower it to soften the image.
    • Blend with the color: Multiply or Soft light lets a texture take on the color. Normal shows the image as it is. Multiply only blends with this section’s own background, not the page behind it. On a section with no background color, turn on Background color (#fcf9f4, the page color) and Paper texture, or the section shows white.

    Add a column

    1. Drag Menu Links from the widget list into the dropdown.
    2. Drop it where the column should appear. Widgets sit left to right in order.
    3. Fill in the heading and links.

    Add a list under a column

    Each Menu Links widget is one column with two lists: a top list and an optional bottom list. In Gifts & Gear, Gear is the bottom list of the Apparel column. Last Chance Items is the bottom list of the Fun Items column.

    1. Click the column that should hold the new list.
    2. Open Bottom of column: heading (optional). Type the heading and pick an icon.
    3. Open Bottom of column: links and fill in the links.
    4. Save, then publish.

    To move a list to another column, copy its heading and links into that column, then empty them in the old one. An emptied bottom list needs both its heading and its links cleared.

    Column styles

    Set the style in the column's Style section.

    • Standard column: heading and links.
    • "Shop by" column: a grey column at the left edge.
    • Short list with lines: for the small dropdowns, Our Craft and About.

    Change the products

    1. Click the product block.
    2. Under Show these products, pick one:
      • First products of the category below: in the category's own sort order.
      • Products of the category below: ones ticked Featured first, then the rest, each A to Z. Tick Featured on a product in Products to move it to the front. With no Featured products in the category, all are A to Z. Used for From the Collection in Belt Buckles.
      • The SKUs below, in this order.
    3. Enter the category URL, or up to 6 SKUs in order.

    Prices and photos come from the product. They update on their own.

    Change a banner

    1. Click the banner.
    2. Upload a photo under Photo, or pick a built-in photo.
    3. Use Image focus to choose which part of the photo stays in view.
    4. Change the small heading, title, button text and link under Text and Link.

    Change the agency tiles

    The tiles show the subcategories of Gov. Agency Items. Change them in the admin, not in Page Builder. See Categories below.

    Hide a dropdown

    Remove every widget from it. The menu item then disappears from the live store.

    Edit the footer

    The footer is edited in Page Builder. One edit changes it on every page.

    1. Open Page Builder on any page and scroll to the bottom.
    2. The footer has two drop zones: "Footer · every page" and "Footer bottom · every page".
    3. Click a widget to edit it. Save, then publish.
    WidgetWhat it shows
    Footer AboutName, the line under it, address, phone, email and hours. Leave a field empty to hide it.
    Menu LinksA column of links with a gold heading, for example Shop. The same widget as in the menu.
    Footer BarThe bottom line: up to 4 short items and the copyright. Each item can be light or gold and can link somewhere.

    Type {year} in the copyright line to show the current year. It updates on its own every January.

    To add a column, drag in another Menu Links. The columns sit left to right in order. On smaller screens they wrap onto more rows.

    Edit the home page sections

    Each home page section is a widget. Our widgets are listed under CUSTOM in Page Builder: page widgets first, then the Menu widgets, then the Footer widgets. Click a section in Page Builder to change it.

    Rule for every widget

    Leave a field empty to hide it. An empty button text hides the button. An empty card title hides the card.

    Fewer cards stay the same size. Photo Cards, Info Cards, Icon Cards, Steps, Help Links, The Collection and Blog Posts keep each card as wide as in a full row. With fewer cards, or a short last row, the cards are centred with the same gap between them.

    Images are the exception. Page Builder cannot empty an image once you choose one. Every image has a switch just above it instead. Set it to None to hide the image, the same as leaving it empty. Where the widget has a built-in photo or icon, None shows that one. Set it back to Show to use your image again.

    Choose the products in The Collection

    1. Click the section.
    2. Open Product SKUs.
    3. Under Show these products, pick one:
      • First 4 of the category: the first 4 products of the category in Category URL.
      • The 4 SKUs below, in this order: type 4 SKUs.
    4. Find a SKU on the product page, or in Products in the admin.

    If a SKU is wrong, Page Builder shows a note. Customers do not see it.

    Choose the featured products

    The Featured carousel shows the products ticked as Featured. No links are needed.

    1. Go to Products and open a product.
    2. Turn on Featured product and save.
    3. The product appears in the carousel. Turn it off to remove it.

    To show a category or a list of SKUs instead, click the carousel in Page Builder. Open Products and change Show these products.

    Quick add adds a product straight to the cart. If the product has options, such as a passcode or a metal, Quick add opens the product's options first.

    Blog posts on the home page

    The Blog Posts section shows your newest blog posts. It updates on its own when you publish a post. Each card uses the post's title, date, first tag and summary.

    1. Go to Storefront › Blog and open a post.
    2. Upload a Thumbnail. Use a landscape photo, at least 960 pixels wide. The card shows it.
    3. Save.

    A post without a thumbnail shows the section's fallback photo. To change it, click the section in Page Builder and open Cards.

    To show other posts, open Posts and change Show these posts. Pick the newest posts with a tag, or type up to 6 post addresses, such as /blog/scammers-in-full-force/.

    Newsletter signups

    The Newsletter section adds visitors to your email list. Choose where signups go under Signup form › Send signups to:

    • BigCommerce newsletter list: signups appear in Marketing › Email marketing › Subscribers.
    • Mailchimp: signups go straight to your Mailchimp audience. In Mailchimp, open Audience › Signup forms › Embedded forms. Copy the address after action=" in the form code, and paste it into Mailchimp form address.

    After a change, sign up once with your own email to check it arrives.

    The Newsletter shows on every page. It sits in the drop zone Above footer · every page, so place it there once, from any page in Page Builder. Don't add a second one to a page.

    Signup form › First name box shows a first name box before the email. Set it to None to ask for the email only.

    Edit text typed on the page

    The text under each section's heading is typed on the page itself, not in the sidebar. This is the same in every widget, including the Photo Feature point text, the Info Cards note and the Story & FAQs story.

    • Click the text and type.
    • Press Enter for a new paragraph.
    • Select words to make them bold or italic, or to add a link, from the small toolbar.

    An empty text box shows as a dashed outline in Page Builder. Click inside it to type. It doesn't show on the store.

    Change the video

    1. Open the video on YouTube and copy its address, for example https://youtu.be/bxDfetbATdI.
    2. Click the Craft Video section in Page Builder and open Video.
    3. Paste the address into YouTube address.

    The video loads only when a visitor presses play. Until then, YouTube's own cover image shows. To use a different cover, upload one under Cover image.

    Trust Bar icons

    Upload an icon under Icon image. Use a square, one-color PNG or SVG with a clear background. It shows in tan. For a full-color image, pick Show as uploaded or pasted. With no upload, pick an icon from Built-in icon.

    Every icon list in every widget has the same 72 icons, from A to Z. That covers the Trust Bar, Icon Cards, Help Links, Craft Video, Photo Feature and Menu Links headings. See Icons for a picture of each one.

    To use an SVG file as code instead, open it in a text editor, copy everything from <svg to </svg>, and tick Use SVG code for the icon, and paste it into the SVG code box that opens. The box is one line high; the whole code goes in it. The pasted icon replaces the image and the built-in icon. Untick the box to go back to them. It shows in tan unless you pick Show as uploaded or pasted. Icon Cards have the same image upload, checkbox and Icon color choice. Craft Video items, Photo Feature points and Menu Links headings have the same checkbox. There it always shows in the icon color.

    SVGs from icon sites such as Icons8 or Google Fonts are already on one line. If an SVG file has its code on several lines, the line breaks are dropped when you paste. If the icon then doesn't show, open the file in a text editor, join the lines with a space between them, and paste again.

    If an SVG comes out as a solid block, it was saved with colors in a style block. In Illustrator, export it again with Styling: Presentation Attributes.

    Build a full-width page

    A web page can be built from widgets, full width, like the home page. "Design a Custom Belt Buckle" is built this way.

    1. Create the page in Storefront › Web Pages, or in Page Builder. Leave the page's own text box empty. A page with no text of its own is full width automatically.
    2. Open the page in Page Builder. Drag widgets into the drop zone that fills the page, Page Builder Content · this page.
    3. Start with a Hero. Leave Layout › Height on Short and set Text › Heading level to H1. The Hero heading is the page title.

    The breadcrumbs sit on top of the Hero. They follow the page's parent page. To show Home / Custom Orders / the page, set the parent page to Custom Orders.

    Leave Template Layout File on default. Widgets belong to the layout they were placed in. Changing the layout file hides them from the page. They aren't deleted: set it back to default and they return.

    Photo Feature settings to know:

    • Layout › Photo or video: put the photo on the left or the right. Alternate them down the page.
    • Text › Text color: Dark on light backgrounds, Light (white) on dark ones.
    • Video: paste a YouTube address to show the video instead of the photo. Set Button 1 › Icon to Play and the button starts the video.
    • Point 1–4: type the title in the sidebar and the text under it on the page. To add point 2, 3 or 4, tick Show point 2 (or 3, 4). Untick it to remove the point. Pick None as the icon for a point without one.

    Photo Feature, Info Cards, Steps and Tag Links have Text color at the top of the heading section (Text in Photo Feature). Pick Light on a dark background.

    Info Cards: the text under the heading follows Heading position (centred or left). For a note with a rust line beside it, as in "Finishes & extras", tick Heading › Add a note with a rust line and type the note on the page. Pick Layout › Card style › Outlined on a dark background. Leave a card's title empty to hide the card.

    More Info Cards settings:

    • Layout › Photo shape: Standard (4:3) or Wide (3:2), as in the Customized Items cards.
    • Card › Dotted link text: shows a dotted link at the bottom of the card, such as "See coins & badges". It goes to the card's Link. Leave it empty for no dotted link.
    • Link below the cards: one arrow link centred under all the cards, such as "Shop your agency". Leave the text empty to hide it.

    Chapter settings to know:

    • Layout › Year and heading: On one line above puts the year and heading across the top, as in 1976.

    Frequently bought together ("Complete the look")

    The "Complete the look" section on a product page shows the product with a few others that go with it, for example a buckle with a belt and a bail. Shoppers tick the ones they want and click Add all to cart.

    You turn it on for one product at a time, with a custom field on that product:

    Custom fieldWhat to typeExample
    NameExactly __freq_bought_together: two underscores at the start, then the words joined by single underscores. All lower case.__freq_bought_together
    ValueThe ID numbers of the products to show with it, separated by commas. Numbers and commas only, no spaces.376,501

    Step 1. Find the ID of each product to add.

    1. Go to Products › View and open the product, for example the belt.
    2. Look at the page address. The ID is the number at the end, for example …/products/edit/376 is ID 376.
    3. Write down the ID. Do the same for each product to add.

    Step 2. Add the custom field to the main product.

    1. Open the product that gets the section, for example the buckle.
    2. Scroll to Custom Fields and click Add Custom Field.
    3. In Custom field name, type __freq_bought_together.
    4. In Custom field value, type the IDs, for example 376,501.
    5. Click Save.

    Step 3. Check the product page. Open the product on the store in a private window. "Complete the look" shows under the product, with the product first (labeled This item) and the others after it (labeled Add-on).

    Good to know:

    • Shoppers don't see the custom field. Fields whose name starts with two underscores are hidden on the product page.
    • The products you add must be visible in the store. A hidden or deleted product is left out.
    • The link only goes one way. To show the section on the belt too, give the belt its own __freq_bought_together field.
    • To change the products, edit the field's value. To remove the section, delete the field.
    • The section heading and labels are the same on every product.
    • If the section doesn't show, check the name first. A missing underscore, a capital letter or a space stops it.

    1. Change the name, parent or URL.
    2. Tick Create redirect. Old links then lead to the new address.

    • Type the phone number the way it should look, for example 800-303-5703. Visitors on phones can tap it to call.
    • For a page on this store, type only the part after the domain, for example /contact-us/.
    • Leave a field empty to hide that item. The tagline shows on wide screens only.
    • Ignore the Top Bar Promotion settings below it. This theme doesn't use them.

    Promo banners

    A promo banner is a strip of text at the top or bottom of the page, for example a sale or a shipping deadline. Add one in Marketing › Banners.

    Banners show rust with light text, centered, and links are underlined. The text is always centered, even if you pick another alignment in the banner editor. Type plain text; don't set a color in the banner editor unless you want a different one.
